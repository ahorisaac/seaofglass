DNS & Whois

18 minReading Lab: pending Quiz: pending

Lesson complete — progress saved.

DNS is the internet’s phonebook. Whois is the registration card. Together they map a target before you ever send a payload.

name → record → address or clue

DNS turns human names into machine answers. Whois tells you who registered a domain, when, and which name servers serve it. Both are passive reconnaissance: you learn structure without exploiting anything.

Record Meaning Example Recon value
A Name → IPv4 glasscorp.io → 10.10.14.2 Finds the web server.
AAAA Name → IPv6 glasscorp.io → 2001:db8::14 IPv6 exposure is often forgotten.
CNAME Alias → another name shop → shops.example-cdn.net Reveals hosting/CDN providers.
MX Mail servers 10 mail.glasscorp.io Mail infrastructure and phishing surface.
TXT Free text v=spf1 mx ~all SPF/DKIM/DMARC and verification clues.
NS Authoritative name servers ns1.glasscorp.io Where the zone lives.

Whois reveals…

Registrar, creation/expiry dates, name servers and status flags — sometimes registrant details, though privacy redaction is common. Use it to understand ownership and infrastructure, not to harass.
