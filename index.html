01 // About us

Clarity in a sea of noise.

Security education is either shallow videos or thousand-page documents. Shidoon built Sea of Glass to be neither: every concept rendered as a living 3D model, every lesson armed with a real terminal, every attack taught from both sides of the wire.

    No black boxes. You see how every attack works, packet by packet, before you ever run it.

    nmap, Burp, Metasploit, hashcat — practised in isolated labs on Kali & Parrot, never on real targets.
